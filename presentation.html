StatSkill AI — Competency Layer for iGOT Karmayogi

Team AIZENIX • ID: 176704 • Idea ID: 21121
1. Role-Calibrated Capability Profiling
Profiles each officer (role, seniority, past training) and scores competency across 4 core domains (Statistical Methods, Technical Analytics, Governance/DPDP 2023, and Behavioural Leadership) to find exact skill gaps with deterministic zero-hallucination scoring.
2. Closed-Loop Remediation Cycle
Recommends personalized iGOT / NSSTA-TPAC courses, tracks completion via iGOT APIs, and re-scores after learning in a continuous: Measure → Diagnose → Learn → Reassess loop.
3. Flagship AI MCQ Generator & Document Intelligence
Training managers upload reference manuals (PDF/DOCX/PPT) → AI extracts concepts and generates trainer-reviewed MCQs with explanations. Sits ON TOP of iGOT as an intelligence layer, not a replacement.

Feasibility, Viability & Mitigation Strategy

Team AIZENIX • ID: 176704 • Idea ID: 21121
✔ Technical Feasibility
• MCQ Generation: Built using LLM and NLP document parsing technology.

• Competency Scoring: Pure JavaScript algorithmic engine guaranteeing 100% mathematical consistency without hallucinations.

• Course Recommendations: Automated mapping of deficiency vectors (Gap = Target - Current) to iGOT modules.

• Lightweight Footprint: <500KB client bundle optimized for low-bandwidth field deployment.
🛡️ Challenges & Institutional Solutions
• Challenge: No direct access to iGOT production APIs.
  ↳ Solution: Start with mock/sandbox iGOT data; transition to live gateway via institutional MoU.

• Challenge: Officer data privacy and security concerns.
  ↳ Solution: DPDP Act 2023 compliance, tokenized auth, zero plaintext credential storage.

• Challenge: Accurate cadre competency taxonomy.
  ↳ Solution: Calibrated with MoSPI/NSSTA expert benchmarks.
